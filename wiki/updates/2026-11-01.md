## Lines to Genghis Khan

- 2026-02 — **PNAS: "Genomes of the Golden Horde elites and their implications for the rulers of the Mongol Empire."** Four individuals from Ulytau mausoleums, Kazakhstan, including the one traditionally attributed to Jochi. Three males paternally related, carrying a rare branch of C3* (C2). Why it matters: first ancient C3* in a Chinggisid-tradition burial, but the branch's rarity today undercuts the "1 in 200 men" star-cluster attribution. Jochi identification not confirmed. Dating reported inconsistently across coverage (13th vs 14th century; one individual 18th century). **[A]** https://www.pnas.org/doi/abs/10.1073/pnas.2531003123
- 2026-03-07/08 — Press framing (Greek Reporter, Gigazine, ZME Science, All That's Interesting): "fewer people related to Genghis Khan than thought"; "closer to finding the tomb". **[C]**
- Reconfirmed background for the page: Tavan Tolgoi R1b (2016), Hebei Q, Wei et al. 2018 C2-F3796 vs C2-M407, Lu clan C2-F1756.
