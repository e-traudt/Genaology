# Methodology

## Evidence grades

Every substantive claim on a topic page carries one of these tags.

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[A]** | Peer-reviewed paper, primary document, or lineage-society-verified line with citations. |
| **[B]** | Preprint, conference presentation, established scholarly reference (e.g. Medieval Lands, Richardson's *Royal Ancestry*), or company release with stated methods. |
| **[C]** | Reputable journalism, expert blog (e.g. DNAeXplained, Genea-Musings), or company marketing with no stated methods. |
| **[D]** | Popular claim, user-contributed tree (Geni, WikiTree, FamilySearch), or tradition. Recorded for tracking, not endorsement. |

- bioRxiv (Genetics, Evolutionary Biology), Wikipedia "2026 in bioarchaeology" as a tripwire

**Genealogy reference**

- Order of the Crown of Charlemagne in America, Baronial Order of Magna Charta, Society of the Descendants of the Illegitimate Sons and Daughters of the Kings of Britain

**Industry**
- Ancestry blog, 23andMe blog and press, MyHeritage blog, FamilyTreeDNA blog and haplotree, Living DNA, Nebula, GEDmatch, DNA Painter, YFull, ISOGG wiki

- Conferences: RootsTech (Feb/Mar), ASHG (Oct), i4GG, ISBA, SAA, EAA

**Forensic / policy**
- DOJ interim policy on FGG, state legislation trackers (Maryland, Montana, Utah and new bills), GEDmatch and FTDNA opt-in policy changes
- Othram DNASolves, Bode Technology, Parabon, DNA Doe Project, Ramapo College IGG Center case pages
- NIJ and SWGDAM publications on IGG and forensic WGS; appellate rulings on IGG evidence; Forensic Magazine, Forensic Genealogy News, The Legal Genealogist

## Weekly update checklist

1. Search each track for items dated within the last 7–10 days (papers, preprints, releases, verified lines, policy).
2. Add every new item to `wiki/updates/YYYY-MM-DD.md` with date, source link, grade and a one-line "why it matters".
3. Revise the relevant topic page in place. Keep the "Current consensus" section accurate. Move superseded claims to the "History of the question" section rather than deleting them.
4. Update `Home.md` headlines and the "Last weekly pass" date.
5. Review `Watchlist.md`: remove items that landed, add new pending ones.
6. If nothing changed on a track, say so explicitly in the update file.

## Conventions

- Dates are ISO (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Haplogroup names: give both ISOGG long form and terminal SNP where known (e.g. C2b1a3a1-F3796; older literature calls the clade C3*).
- "Documented descent" means a sourced generation-by-generation line. "Statistical descent" means a population-genetics argument. Never conflate them.
