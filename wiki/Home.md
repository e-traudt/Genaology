## Headlines from the latest pass (2026-11-01)

1. **Genghis Khan:** A February 2026 PNAS paper on Golden Horde elite burials at Ulytau, Kazakhstan (including the mausoleum traditionally assigned to Jochi) found three paternally related men carrying a rare branch of haplogroup C3* (C2). The branch is very rare today, which weakens the claim that the widespread C3* "star cluster" descends solely from Genghis Khan. See [Genghis-Khan.md](Genghis-Khan.md).
2. **DNA testing:** AncestryDNA's 2026 Origins update went live on 8 October 2026 with a new region-assignment algorithm, a reference panel above 295,000 samples and a "Regions by Grandparent" feature. MyHeritage is now selling DNA 360°, a 30x whole-genome sequencing kit. FamilyTreeDNA's public Y haplotree passed 100,000 branches around RootsTech 2026. See [DNA-Testing.md](DNA-Testing.md).
3. **Charlemagne:** No new peer-reviewed work. Nathaniel Lane Taylor's January 2025 paper on the Merovingian ancestry of Charlemagne remains the most recent substantive scholarly treatment of his ancestry. Popular pieces in October 2026 recycled the Ralph–Coop / Rohde "everyone descends from him" arguments. See [Charlemagne.md](Charlemagne.md).
4. **Julius Caesar:** No change. There is still no documented biological descent from Caesar, and no accepted "descent from antiquity" line reaching the Julii. Roman-era ancient DNA continues to accumulate but does not touch the Julian family. See [Julius-Caesar.md](Julius-Caesar.md).
5. **Forensic genealogy:** Whole-genome sequencing is now the default for degraded remains (Othram, Bode). Maryland's SB0678 amends the state's IGG authorisation rules with an effective date of 1 October 2026. New October cases include the Ottawa County, Ohio, 1975 Jane Doe and a Ramapo IGG Center referral for Atlantic City John Doe. See [Forensic-Genealogy.md](Forensic-Genealogy.md).

## Quick orientation

|---|---|---|
| Can I document a line to Charlemagne? | Yes, if you can reach a recognised "gateway ancestor". Thousands of lines are accepted by lineage societies. | [Charlemagne.md](Charlemagne.md#documented-descent) |
| Can DNA prove descent from Charlemagne? | No. His DNA has never been recovered, and autosomal DNA from 40 generations back is undetectable. | [Charlemagne.md](Charlemagne.md#dna-and-charlemagne) |
| Are there living descendants of Julius Caesar? | None documented. His bloodline is extinct in the record. Statistical arguments only. | [Julius-Caesar.md](Julius-Caesar.md) |
| Is 1 in 200 men descended from Genghis Khan? | Doubtful. The 2003 estimate rested on an assumption that is now contested by ancient DNA. | [Genghis-Khan.md](Genghis-Khan.md#the-star-cluster-claim) |
| Can my DNA kit help identify remains or suspects? | Only if it is on GEDmatch or FamilyTreeDNA with law-enforcement matching opted in. The big three consumer databases refuse. | [Forensic-Genealogy.md](Forensic-Genealogy.md) |
| Which test for deep paternal ancestry? | FamilyTreeDNA Big Y-700 remains the standard; whole-genome kits (MyHeritage DNA 360°, Nebula, Sequencing.com) are maturing. | [DNA-Testing.md](DNA-Testing.md) |
