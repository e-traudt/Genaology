# Genealogy Developments Wiki

A weekly-updated wiki tracking new developments in genealogy, with a focus on five tracks:

| Track | Page | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Lines to Charlemagne | [wiki/Charlemagne.md](wiki/Charlemagne.md) | Documented descents, gateway ancestors, lineage societies, population-genetics arguments, Carolingian ancient DNA |
| DNA testing | [wiki/DNA-Testing.md](wiki/DNA-Testing.md) | Consumer tests, Y/mtDNA haplotrees, ancient DNA tools, forensic genetic genealogy, privacy and regulation |
| Lines to Julius Caesar | [wiki/Julius-Caesar.md](wiki/Julius-Caesar.md) | The "descent from antiquity" problem, the Julii, Roman-era ancient DNA, claimed lines and their status |
| Lines to Genghis Khan | [wiki/Genghis-Khan.md](wiki/Genghis-Khan.md) | The Y-chromosome "star cluster" debate, Chinggisid burials and ancient DNA, documented Borjigin descents |
| Forensic genealogy | [wiki/Forensic-Genealogy.md](wiki/Forensic-Genealogy.md) | Investigative genetic genealogy cases, forensic whole-genome sequencing, database access, state and federal regulation, case law |

Start at **[wiki/Home.md](wiki/Home.md)**. Each week's new findings are logged in [wiki/updates/](wiki/updates/) and the topic pages are revised in place.

## How updates work

- The wiki is refreshed once a week (every Monday, 1:47 pm Eastern) by an automated research pass that searches for new papers, preprints, product releases, lineage-society publications and reputable news on the five tracks, then commits a dated update file plus any topic-page edits.
- Every claim carries an evidence grade (see [wiki/Methodology.md](wiki/Methodology.md)). Commercial and blog sources are labelled as such.
- Manual edits are welcome. The weekly pass preserves them and adds to them.
